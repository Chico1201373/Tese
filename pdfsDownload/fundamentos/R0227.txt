                                                                                                                                                                                                    healthcare. As of writing this, more than 500 AI algorithms

                                                                                                                                                                                                    the fact that the FDA cleared the first AI algorithm in 1995,

                                                                                                                                                                                                    witnessed only recently, with over 300 AI products receiving
